The still hour

Étale is the slack of the tide, the few minutes when the sea stops moving and the surface turns to glass. We build yachts for that moment.

Electric under way, silent at anchor, and drawn so low and long that the reflection becomes half of the design.

Half of every yacht is its reflection.
